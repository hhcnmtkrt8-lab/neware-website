Applications

The experiment decides the configuration.

Four application areas, each with the test method, the auxiliary hardware and the instrument that fits. Where a method has a known source of error, we say so.

A1 · MATERIALS RESEARCH

The first fifty cycles decide everything.

Coin and half cells are tested at currents where a single range cannot resolve both the charge plateau and the trickle at the end of discharge. Four ranges keep resolution proportional to the signal, and the auxiliary channels log the chamber temperature on the same time base.

MethodsGITT · PITT · CV · galvanostatic cycling
Typical current10 µA – 5 mA
InstrumentCT-4008Tn-5V50mA · CT-9000-5V15A
Critical specAccuracy at range 1, not at full scale
Common errorReading ±0.02% of a 15 A range as 0.02% of the measured value
A2 · SOLID-STATE & PRESSURE

Pressure is a variable, not a fixture detail.

Solid-state cells change thickness as they cycle. If stack pressure is held by a spring, the force drifts and the impedance drift is attributed to the material. A pressure-controlled clamp with a logged load cell turns that into a measured variable you can plot.

MethodsPressure-controlled cycling · EIS-ready fixtures
Range0.1 – 2 t (fixture dependent)
InstrumentCT-9000 + PTF pressure clamp
Logged channelsCurrent · voltage · force · temperature
Common errorForce logged on a separate clock from the electrical data
A3 · FAST CHARACTERISATION

A slow transition hides the ohmic drop.

DCIR and HPPC separate the instantaneous resistance from the polarisation that follows it. If the current transition takes milliseconds, the two merge and the extracted resistance depends on the fit window rather than the cell.

MethodsDCIR · HPPC · pulse trains · 10 s / 30 s steps
Transition≤150 µs (9000) · ≤1.5 ms (4000)
LoggingUp to 10 kHz per channel
InstrumentCT-9000-5V15A · CE-6000
Common errorSampling interval longer than the pulse width
A4 · FORMATION & GRADING

When formation data has to mean something.

Formation is usually run on cost-optimised equipment and then compared against research data from a different class of instrument. Sharing the same front end and the same export schema removes that discontinuity — and makes traceability a data property rather than a procedure.

MethodsFormation · grading · OCV/IR sorting
Channel count8 – 160 per cabinet
InstrumentCE-4000 · CT-4000 high-density racks
TraceabilityPer-channel audit trail, timestamped
Common errorDifferent accuracy class between R&D and production
