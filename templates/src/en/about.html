Company

A measurement company, not a catalogue.

We configure, calibrate and support one product family: battery test systems built for laboratories that publish. The engineering behind them has been in production since 1998.

01 — Who we are

Focused by design.

Most instrument catalogues grow sideways: a cycler, a potentiostat, a chamber, a module tester, a full production line. The result is a brochure nobody can compare and a service organisation stretched across product classes.

Cellaris exists to do the opposite. One measurement architecture, four platforms, and a configuration conversation that starts with the experiment. If a requirement falls outside that scope, we say so.

02 — Manufacturing & disclosure

Who builds the instrument.

Cellaris instruments are engineered and manufactured by Neware Technology, founded in 1998 and in continuous production of battery test systems since then. Cellaris specifies the configuration, the calibration regime and the documentation set for research and industrial laboratories outside China.
