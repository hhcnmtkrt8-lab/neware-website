APPLICATION NOTE · AN-014

What ±0.02% F.S. actually buys you at microamp currents

Published 2026 · Cellaris Instruments · applies to CT-4000 and CT-9000 series

The claim, and the arithmetic behind it

Accuracy expressed as a percentage of full scale is only meaningful when the range is stated. On a single 15 A channel, ±0.02% F.S. equals ±3 mA. That is a perfectly good specification for a power cell and a useless one for the tail of a coin-cell discharge, where the current may fall below 100 µA.

A four-range front end changes the denominator. On the CT-9000-5V15A, range 1 spans 15 µA to 15 mA; ±0.02% of that range is ±3 µA. The same instrument, in the same test, resolves three orders of magnitude more detail than a single-range channel — not because the electronics are different, but because the range is.

Where the boundary matters

Auto-ranging is only useful if the transition is clean. A range change that introduces a settling transient will appear as a step in the recorded current at exactly the moment the experiment crosses the boundary — usually during the voltage hold at the end of charge, which is where coulombic efficiency is calculated.

Two practical checks when evaluating any instrument:

  • Run a slow ramp through a range boundary and plot the current derivative. A discontinuity indicates a settling artefact rather than cell behaviour.
  • Compare the integrated charge on either side of the boundary against a reference. If the total drifts, the transition is contributing error.

What this does not fix

Range architecture does not correct a current offset, a temperature gradient between the cell and the sensor, or a cable resistance that sits inside the voltage sense path. Those are installation and calibration issues and they dominate at microamp levels. A four-range instrument gives you the resolution to see them; it does not remove them.

Test conditions for the figures in this note: 2032 coin cell, LiFePO₄ cathode, 1 M LiPF₆ in EC/DEC, 25 °C chamber, C/20 formation then C/2 cycling. Instrument configuration: CT-9000-5V15A, range 1, 10 Hz logging.
