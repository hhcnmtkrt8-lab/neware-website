03 — Measurement architecture

Why four ranges matter more than one number.

A single 15 A range cannot resolve 30 µA. Four auto-switching ranges keep the resolution proportional to the signal — which is the difference between a plausible curve and a publishable one.

CT-9000-5V15A · ±0.02% F.S. per range · ±3 µA on range 1

One chain from sensor to SQL.

Current and voltage are measured on the same time base as auxiliary temperature, chamber state and cell-level taps — then written straight to a database you can query.

  • Four ranges Auto-switching, no manual range selection, no discontinuity at the boundary.
  • 150 µs Current response (10–90% F.S.), so pulse and HPPC steps are not smoothed away.
  • 10 kHz Maximum logging rate on the 9000 series; 10 Hz is enough for long cycle life.
  • Open schema CSV, XLSX, SQL Server, PostgreSQL and a documented REST API.
