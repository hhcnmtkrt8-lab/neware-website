03 — What we do not claim

The limits, stated up front.

  • We do not claim identical measurement results to any other platform. Two instruments can both be accurate and still disagree inside their uncertainty bands.
  • We do not supply a high-end potentiostat for impedance spectroscopy as a standalone product; we integrate with EIS hardware rather than replace it.
  • We do not offer a global on-site service organisation on the scale of the largest premium vendors. We offer regional calibration partners and documented intervals.
  • We do not publish competitor pricing. Configurations are not comparable until channel count, fixtures and software are included.
04 — Where we do win

Three defensible advantages.

  • Range architecture. Four auto-switching ranges on the entry model, not only the flagship.
  • Data freedom. No per-seat licence, documented API, direct database access.
  • Total cost of ownership. Fixtures, spare channels and calibration are priced as accessories, not as a service contract.
