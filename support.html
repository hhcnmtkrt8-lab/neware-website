03 — FAQ

Six questions, answered plainly.

Can I run my existing test programs?
Schedules are imported from a documented format. For migrations from another platform, we translate a representative program and compare the first cycles before the full set is converted.
What happens to my archive if I stop using the software?
CSV and XLSX exports are plain files; the SQL schema is documented. The archive does not require a licence to read.
Do you integrate with third-party chambers and EIS hardware?
Yes — chambers via standard interfaces, and EIS instruments alongside the cycler rather than as a replacement. The auxiliary channels log on the same time base.
What is the warranty?
One year on the instrument, with the calibration interval documented. Extended coverage is quoted as an option, not bundled.
Can I buy channels in stages?
Yes. Cabinets are configured by module; a 16-channel start can be expanded without replacing the controller.
Which markets do you ship to?
North and South America, Europe and selected CIS markets through local partners. Export documentation is prepared per destination.
